Create a stylized icon illustration 
representing STAKES — a daily morning 
commitment feature in a productivity 
app called GOATflow.

CONCEPT:
A wooden stake or post driven firmly 
into the ground at a slight angle —
the visual of something planted, 
immovable, and committed to.
The stake has a small scroll or 
parchment rolled around it near 
the top — the day's commitment, 
nailed to the stake like a declaration.
The scroll has a small red wax seal 
with a hoof print stamp on it.

This communicates: 
A promise made. A contract signed.
Something driven into the ground 
that is not moving until it is honored.

THE STAKE:
Wooden post, rough-hewn texture,
driven into rocky terrain at 
a slight diagonal angle —
suggesting it was planted with 
force and intention, not placed gently.
The wood grain is visible — 
this is not decorative, 
this is structural.
Color: warm wood brown with 
dark outline — same illustration 
weight as rest of GOATflow icon set.
A small gold/amber nail or pin 
secures the scroll to the stake —
the same gold (#f59e0b) used 
throughout the GOATflow brand.

THE SCROLL:
Small rolled parchment wrapped 
around the upper portion of the stake.
Warm cream/ivory color — 
same warmth as the GOATflow 
mascot's fur (#f0ece0).
The scroll is partially unrolled —
one edge curling outward slightly,
suggesting it was just signed 
and freshly committed to.
A small red wax seal at the 
bottom edge of the scroll 
with a tiny hoof print stamped 
into it — the GOATflow signature mark.
The scroll itself is blank — 
it represents the user's commitment,
whatever they have chosen to stake.

ATMOSPHERE:
Black background #000000.
A warm amber/gold radial glow 
(#f59e0b at 20% opacity) 
behind the stake — 
the glow of something 
that matters, something 
with consequences attached.
This is the warmest, 
most grounded icon in the set —
Stakes is not about energy or power,
it is about accountability and weight.
Two or three small white 
4-point sparkle stars 
near the scroll — 
the quiet satisfaction of 
a commitment made.
A very subtle ground line 
at the base of the stake —
just enough terrain texture 
to suggest the stake is 
driven into something real.

VISUAL STYLE:
Match the warm cartoon side 
of the GOATflow icon set —
same family as the Hay bale, 
Fresh Cheese, and Trail Notes icons.
Bold dark outlines throughout.
Flat cartoon with subtle shading.
Black background.
Same illustration weight as rest of set.
The scroll and stake together 
should feel like a game item —
like something you would find 
in an RPG quest log, 
a binding contract from 
a fantasy merchant,
the physical representation 
of a promise that has 
real in-game consequences.

COMPOSITION:
Square format.
Stake centered, driven into 
ground at bottom of frame,
filling approximately 60% 
of frame height.
The stake angles 5-10 degrees 
to the right — planted with 
intention, not perfectly vertical.
Scroll wrapped around upper third 
of the stake, partially unfurled.
Gold glow behind the stake center.
Sparkle stars in upper corners.
Ground texture at very bottom edge.

SIZE PERFORMANCE:
At large size: a wooden stake 
driven into rocky ground with 
a sealed scroll attached — 
reads as commitment, contract,
accountability, weight.
At 22px stat card size: 
a vertical stake shape with 
a small rectangle attached —
reads as a marker or flag,
immediately distinct from 
every other stat icon.
Paired with STAKE label:
instantly understood as 
the daily commitment tracker.

WHAT IT COMMUNICATES:
You planted something today.
It is in the ground.
It is not moving until 
you honor it.
That is the Stake.

Do not include text or labels.
