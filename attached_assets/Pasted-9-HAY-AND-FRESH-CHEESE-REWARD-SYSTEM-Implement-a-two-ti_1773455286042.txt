9. HAY AND FRESH CHEESE REWARD SYSTEM

Implement a two-tier reward currency system that operates 
as follows:

HAY — earned by completing Tracks:
- Standard Track completion: +10 Hay
- Summit Call completion: +50 Hay  
- Any Track completed within 24 hours of being generated: 
  +10 Hay speed bonus (stacks with base reward)

HAY TO FRESH CHEESE CONVERSION:
- Every 500 Hay automatically converts to 1 Fresh Cheese
- Conversion triggers automatically when threshold is reached
- On conversion, display a brief celebration animation:
  a small pile of hay transforming into a glowing cheese 
  wedge, 2 seconds, centered overlay on the dashboard
- Play a subtle success sound on conversion if audio 
  is enabled
- Display a conversion notification card in the 
  Track feed: "500 Hay converted to 1 Fresh Cheese. 
  Keep going."

DASHBOARD STAT CARDS — update the four existing stat 
cards to the following five cards in this order:

1. ACTIVE TRACKS (white number, existing style)
2. SUMMIT CALLS (red number, urgent styling)  
3. COMPLETED (white number)
4. HAY (amber/gold number — #f59e0b — with a small 
   hay bale emoji or icon: 🌾)
5. FRESH CHEESE (green number — #22c55e — with a 
   small cheese emoji or icon: 🧀)

Display both the current Hay balance and a progress 
indicator showing proximity to next Fresh Cheese 
conversion. Format: "340 / 500 HAY" so the user 
always knows how close they are.

FRESH CHEESE TOTAL — display the user's total 
accumulated Fresh Cheese prominently in the 
user profile side panel under "My Stats":
- Label: "Fresh Cheese Banked"
- Value in green (#22c55e)
- Beneath the value, display in small muted text:
  "Ports to WorkGOAT when available — workgoat.vip"
  Make "workgoat.vip" a clickable link.

PASTURE GAUGE — update the gauge label to reflect 
both Hay progress and Cheese Churn Rate (CCR):
- Primary label: current tier and fence threshold 
  (e.g. "The Pen → 100/5,000 CCR")
- Secondary label beneath: 
  "340/500 Hay to next Fresh Cheese"

HAY DECAY — do not implement decay on Hay in V1. 
Hay is a simple accumulation currency at this stage. 
Decay mechanics can be introduced in a later version 
once the WorkGOAT port is live and the full Cheese 
decay system applies. Flag this in the codebase with 
a comment: // HAY DECAY — reserved for WorkGOAT 
integration phase

WORKGOAT PORT BUTTON — in the user profile panel, 
beneath the Fresh Cheese Banked stat, add a button 
labeled "Port to WorkGOAT" styled as a secondary 
button (transparent, purple border). For now this 
button should display a tooltip on hover or click 
that reads: "WorkGOAT is coming. Your Fresh Cheese 
will be waiting." Do not make it functional yet — 
just plant the flag visually so users understand 
the connection from day one.
